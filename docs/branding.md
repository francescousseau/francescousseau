# Branding

## Positioning

AI Engineer · Data Scientist · BI Consultant, with a Business Administration and Logistics background. The differentiator is connecting technical choices to a measurable business outcome. Seniority is not claimed beyond what the public repositories show.

**Value proposition (EN):** I turn data and AI into decision-ready solutions, from clean data and validated models to the business cost of every error.

## Taglines (recommended: 1)

1. Bridging Artificial Intelligence, Data Science and Business Strategy.
2. From data to decisions: applied ML with business-cost evidence.
3. Applied AI and data science, measured in business outcomes.
4. Data quality, honest validation, decision-ready results.
5. AI engineering with a business-first mindset.

Option 1 is the one in the banner and works on both the profile and LinkedIn.
